🎬 Production Notes

  • Visual rhythm: Fast cuts (1.5–3s). UI screen recordings from a real ClickUp workspace. No stock footage.
  • Voiceover: Mid-range, confident, ~160 words/min. Slight reverb on key phrases ("Brain 2", "Super Agents").
  • Transitions: Scene 1→2 — fragmented app icons fly together → ClickUp logo. Scene 3→4 — Brain 2 morphs into Agent. Scene 6→7 — graph animates up.
  • Music: Starts sparse (pulse + sub-bass). Builds layer by layer. Full drop at 0:33 (ROI scene). Resolves at 0:56 for CTA.
  • End card: ClickUp logo centered, "Software to replace all software" beneath, two buttons: "Start Free" (primary purple) | "Watch Demo" (outline white).
